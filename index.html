Top 5 European Clothing Manufacturers for Fashion Brands

Looking for a clothing manufacturer in Europe? This guide highlights five European garment manufacturers and production partners for private label clothing, custom apparel, small batches, jerseywear and fashion collections.

Finding the right European clothing manufacturer can be one of the hardest parts of starting or scaling a fashion brand. Minimum order quantities, sampling, private-label options, development services and production lead times vary significantly from one supplier to another.

The five companies below represent different types of European clothing production. Some focus on lower-volume private-label manufacturing, while others are better suited to more established brands that need product development and larger-scale production.

Important: “Top” here does not mean one factory is universally better than another. The best manufacturer depends on your garment type, quantity, target price, materials, timeline and level of customization.

1. White Cotton — Private Label Clothing Manufacturer in Portugal

White Cotton is a clothing manufacturer based in Barcelos, Portugal. It offers private-label and white-label production for brands producing garments such as t-shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, shirts, outerwear, pants and shorts.

One reason it may appeal to emerging fashion brands is its publicly listed minimum order quantities. White Cotton states that heavy garments such as hoodies and sweatshirts can start at 100 pieces in one colourway, or 75 per colourway when ordering two or more colours. Light garments generally begin at higher quantities.

The company handles product development, fabric sourcing, sampling, production, labels, packaging and other branding elements. It also advertises access to options using OEKO-TEX, GOTS organic cotton and Better Cotton materials through partner mills.

2. Anglotex — Portuguese Jerseywear and Activewear Manufacturer

Anglotex is a Portuguese family-run clothing manufacturer founded in 1991. The company operates two factories and specializes primarily in high-quality jerseywear, with additional capabilities in activewear and soft woven garments.

Anglotex says its production operation includes cutting, sewing and packaging in-house and covers menswear, womenswear, childrenswear and babywear. It also offers product development, 3D design and prototyping.

Anglotex reports annual capacity above two million garments and describes an average development lead time of four to six weeks and average production lead time of eight to twelve weeks. It also lists renewed GRS, GOTS and OCS certifications on its website.

How to choose a clothing manufacturer in Europe

A factory that works well for one fashion brand may be completely wrong for another. Before contacting a manufacturer, prepare a clear description of the garment, target quantity, fabrics, sizing, branding requirements and desired delivery date.

Pay close attention to minimum order quantities. A supplier may advertise a garment MOQ while separate minimums apply to custom fabric, woven labels, packaging or trims. Ask manufacturers to explain all minimums before paying for development or samples.

Questions to ask a European garment manufacturer

  • What is the MOQ per style, colour and size?
  • Can you produce a prototype or sample before bulk production?
  • Do you provide private-label labels, hangtags and packaging?
  • Can you source fabric, or must the brand provide it?
  • What is the typical sampling and bulk-production lead time?
  • Which certifications apply to the factory and which apply only to specific fabrics?
  • What payment terms apply to samples and production?
  • Can you provide a written quotation detailing all production costs?

Frequently asked questions

Which European country is best for clothing manufacturing?

There is no single best country. Portugal is well known for knitwear, jerseywear, casualwear and premium apparel production; Italy has a deep luxury and specialist textile supply chain; and countries such as Lithuania can provide flexible garment-development and sewing services. The right choice depends on your product and order size.

Can I find low MOQ clothing manufacturers in Europe?

Yes. Some European factories accept relatively small production runs. However, MOQ can vary by garment, colour, fabric and customization. A low garment MOQ does not necessarily mean custom fabric, labels or packaging have the same minimum.

What is private label clothing manufacturing?

Private label manufacturing means a factory produces garments that are sold under the client’s brand. Depending on the supplier, this can include custom labels, hangtags, packaging, garment development and other branding options.

Should I order a sample before bulk production?

In most cases, sampling is useful before committing to a larger order because it allows you to check fit, fabric, construction, measurements, colours and branding. Ask the manufacturer how its approval process works before bulk production begins.
