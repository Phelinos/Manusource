5 Clothing Manufacturers in Portugal for Fashion Brands

Portugal is one of Europe’s best-known clothing-production hubs. This guide covers five Portuguese clothing manufacturers and production partners offering private-label, custom, streetwear and lower-volume manufacturing options.

Updated September 28, 2026 · Always confirm current MOQs, pricing and timelines directly with each manufacturer.

1. White Cotton

White Cotton is a family-run clothing manufacturer in Barcelos producing private-label and white-label garments for fashion brands. Its range includes hoodies, sweatshirts, t-shirts, shirts, outerwear, pants and shorts.

CountryPortugal
ProductionPrivate label & white label
MOQFrom 75 pcs per colourway on selected garments
SamplesSingle prototypes available

White Cotton publishes relatively detailed MOQ information. Its official private-label page states that hoodies, sweatshirts, zip-ups, sweatpants and shorts can start at 100 units in one colourway or 75 per colour with two or more colourways. T-shirts and other lighter garments generally start higher.

Source: White Cotton official website.

Why clothing brands manufacture in Portugal

Portugal has a mature textile and garment-production ecosystem, particularly in northern regions such as Barcelos, Braga and the surrounding textile corridor. For European brands, producing within the EU can also simplify logistics compared with sourcing from manufacturers much farther away.

That does not mean Portuguese manufacturing is automatically the right option for every business. Compare garment quality, total landed cost, fabric minimums, communication, sampling, production capacity and delivery timing before choosing a supplier.
