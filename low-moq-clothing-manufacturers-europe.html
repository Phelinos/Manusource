Low MOQ Clothing Manufacturers in Europe

Looking for European clothing manufacturers that accept smaller orders? This guide highlights manufacturers and production options for startups, private-label brands and small-batch fashion businesses.

← Back to the Top 5 European Clothing Manufacturers guide

MOQ means minimum order quantity. For a new clothing brand, MOQ can be one of the biggest barriers to production because many factories require hundreds of units per style or colour. Some European manufacturers, however, offer lower thresholds, blanks, or small-batch production routes.

Important: MOQ can vary by garment, fabric, colour, labels and packaging. Always ask for a written quotation before assuming the advertised garment MOQ applies to the whole order.
Portugal

1. White Cotton

White Cotton is a clothing manufacturer in Barcelos, Portugal, focused on custom casualwear, streetwear, private label and white label production. The company publicly advertises some of the lower minimums available among European manufacturers.

Published MOQ100 units in one colour, or 75 per colourway on 2+
Best forHoodies, t-shirts, sweatshirts, casualwear
ProductionPrivate label, white label, cut-and-sew
LocationBarcelos, Portugal

The company says it can handle pattern making, fabric sourcing, sampling, decoration, quality control and packaging, which can be useful for smaller brands that want a single production partner.

Source: White Cotton official website.

ProductionPrivate label, custom manufacturing, blanks

For a brand testing designs before committing to custom production, blanks can provide a lower-risk route into branding and sales.

How to choose a low MOQ clothing manufacturer

Low MOQ is useful, but it should not be the only thing you compare. A factory that accepts 50 units may still be a poor fit if the fabric, construction, quality level or production timing does not match your product.

Before contacting a manufacturer, prepare your target quantity, garment type, reference images or tech pack, preferred fabric, target delivery date and branding requirements. Ask whether the MOQ applies per style, per colour or across the total order.
